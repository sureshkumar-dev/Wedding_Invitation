## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the regal grandeur and spiritual sanctity of a classical South Indian Tamil Hindu wedding through a modern, cinematic digital medium. Built for couples, families, and honored guests, the visual tone reconciles solemn traditional reverence with the refined polish of luxury editorial design. 

The aesthetic is grounded in a blend of **Tactile Luxury** and **Dark Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Sanctum Immersion:** Deep temple obsidian and warm charcoal backdrops evoke the sacred inner halls (garbhagriha) illuminated by bronze agal vilakku oil lamps.
- **Temple Ornamentation:** Architectural balance inspired by Dravidian gopuram carvings, delicate filigree framing, traditional rice-flour kolam geometry, and floral toranam accents.
- **Modern Cinematic Pacing:** Controlled whitespace, high-contrast serif titling, delicate metallic line work, and serene transitions that treat digital invitation cards like bespoke physical keepsakes embossed on hand-laid champagne silk paper.

## Colors

The palette draws strictly from sacred temple elements, metallic craftsmanship, and celebratory South Indian rituals:

- **Primary (`#D4AF37` Antique Gold):** Represents divine prosperity and mangala sutra brilliance. Supported by `#E5C158` (Champagne Gold highlight) and `#997D29` (Aged Brass shade). Used for key celebratory highlights, crests, borders, and active status treatments.
- **Secondary (`#8B1E1E` Sacred Kumkum Vermillion):** An auspicious ritual accent derived from vermillion and saffron paste. Reserved for sacred blessings, urgent calendar milestones (Muhurtham dates), auspicious icons, and high-priority actions.
- **Tertiary (`#997D29` Temple Bronze):** Grounding metallic tone for divider filaments, subtle filigree details, and subdued structural frames.
- **Neutral Surface Hierarchy:**
  - Base Obsidian: `#120F0D` (Canvas / deep dark sanctum)
  - Charcoal Silk: `#1C1613` (Elevated card containers, overlays, structural panels)
  - Warm Bronze Slate: `#28211C` (Interactive hover states and nested sub-cards)
- **Text & Foreground (Champagne Silk / Warm Ivory):**
  - Primary Content: `#FAF6EE` (Warm ivory for primary readability)
  - Secondary Content: `#C8BFAF` (Muted parchment for timestamps, itineraries, and secondary guidance)
  - Subtle Labels: `#8C8176` (Tertiary captions and metadata)

## Typography

Typography establishes an aristocratic, classical cadence reminiscent of traditional wedding patrika invitations while maintaining crisp modern legibility.

- **Headlines & Royal Titles (`Playfair Display`):** High-contrast transitional serifs carry romantic gravity. Uppercase headings must use generous letter-spacing (0.05em to 0.15em).
- **Body & Ceremonial Prose (`EB Garamond`):** Classical, humanistic letterforms provide warm narrative rhythm for personal messages, family lineages, and venue details.
- **System Labels, Badges & Interactive UI (`Manrope`):** Clean, geometric grotesque type provides high legibility at micro sizes (dates, RSVP counters, venue addresses, buttons), contrasting gracefully against the ornate serif headlines.
- **Tamil Typographic Pairing:** For bilingual and Tamil scripts (mantras, invocations, names), employ Noto Serif Tamil matching the weight and optical scale of Playfair Display and EB Garamond.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model honors the symmetry and deliberate proportions of mandapam architecture.

- **Grid System:** An 8-column layout on desktop capped at a max-width of `1040px` to mirror physical royal invitation folios. Adapts to a 4-column layout on mobile devices.
- **Symmetry & Centering:** Core ceremonial details (the couple's monogram, muhurtham timing, prayer verses) default to rigid central alignment, flanked by balanced horizontal dividers and filigree motifs.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Deliberate, unhurried negative space between sections conveys prestige. Significant ceremonial transitions (e.g., from *Nischayathartham* to *Muhurtham* to *Reception*) must use `space-xl` multiplied by 2 (`5rem`) to create natural narrative pauses.
- **Responsive Adaptations:** Desktop cards retain dual-page ceremonial layouts; mobile viewports reflow into a continuous, linear vertical scroll with persistent gold bottom-sheet navigation for RSVPs and venue maps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth reflects sacred oil lamp illumination—soft, warm ambient glows casting subtle golden light across obsidian and stone textures.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base: Deep Temple Obsidian (`#120F0D`).
  - Level 1 (Event Cards & Panels): Charcoal Silk (`#1C1613`) with a 1px antique brass border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`).
  - Level 2 (Modals & RSVP Dialogs): Elevated Charcoal (`#231C18`) with dual-tone brass filigree borders.
- **Ambient Shadows & Glows:**
  - **Temple Lamp Glow:** Rather than cold black drop shadows, elevated elements use warm, tinted ambients: `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 20px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
  - **Kumkum Focus Glow:** Critical action triggers or live Muhurtham countdowns emit a faint crimson aura: `0 0 24px rgba(139, 30, 30, 0.35)`.
- **Ornamental Layering:** Delicate corner motifs (Gopuram-inspired corner notches or floral kolam flourishes) rest directly on the border plane with partial transparency (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`), catching subtle highlights on hover.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, architectural shape language (Level 1). Curves are restrained to preserve the crisp formality of fine stationery and stone temple masonry.

- **Base Corner Radius:** Standard components utilize `0.25rem` (4px) rounding. Large invitation containers and ceremonial cards use `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Architectural Motifs:** Interactive chips, key focal badges, and hero monograms may feature inverted corner brackets or subtle arch contours (mimicking Dravidian koodu / sanctum arches) via CSS clipping paths.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted strictly to micro-status badges (e.g., "Auspicious Time / நன்னாள்") to distinguish functional notifications from stationery cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Royal Gold Leaf):** Solid antique gold background (`#D4AF37`) with dark obsidian text (`#120F0D`), `label-lg` uppercase typography, and subtle inner gold glow. Hover shifts to Champagne (`#E5C158`) with an amplified warm amber drop shadow.
- **Secondary (Temple Outline):** Transparent background, 1px antique gold border (`#D4AF37`), champagne silk text (`#FAF6EE`). Hover triggers an obsidian-tinted warm fill (`#1C1613`) and full border opacity.
- **Ceremonial Accent (Kumkum):** Deep vermillion fill (`#8B1E1E`) with ivory text (`#FFFFFF`) for immediate RSVP confirmations and live streaming links.

### Cards & Itinerary Modules
- Framed with a 1px border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`).
- Decorated with four subtle geometric corner brackets inspired by Dravidian temple beams.
- Interior background set to deep charcoal silk (`#1C1613`) with subtle radial gradients highlighting card centers.

### Input Fields & RSVP Forms
- Minimalist floating fields: bottom-border or subtle enclosing border in bronze (`#997D29`).
- Dark charcoal canvas (`#161210`) with ivory input text (`#FAF6EE`).
- Focus state reveals an antique gold outline (`#D4AF37`) accompanied by a subtle amber glow.

### Chips & Event Filters
- Compact filters for wedding events (e.g., *Vratham*, *Sangeet*, *Muhurtham*, *Reception*).
- Default: Bordered by muted bronze with ivory-muted text (`#C8BFAF`).
- Selected: Charcoal fill with crisp antique gold border and gold text, accompanied by a small vermillion dot indicator.

### Sacred Dividers & Monograms
- Central dividers feature thin horizontal gold hairpins terminating in traditional floral or paisley motifs (Mangai / Kalka).
- Dedicated monogram medallions display coupled initials in Playfair Display enclosed within a double-ring brass mandala.
